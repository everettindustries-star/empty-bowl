# THE EMPTY BOWL — Script v1

Format: 90 seconds, 16:9 (protected 9:16 center crop).
Cutdowns: 30-second and 15-second versions (notes at the end).
Tone: Urban noir crime thriller. Dry documentary narrator. No animal dialogue.

**Locked animal identities (use in every shot prompt):**
- DOG: scruffy medium-sized terrier mix, wiry tan-and-grey coat, floppy ears, soulful brown eyes
- CAT: lean short-haired grey tabby cat, green eyes

---

## Shot 1 — 00:00–00:12 — "The Bowl"

**Visual:** Rain streaks down a dark kitchen window. On the floor below, a stainless
steel bowl sits empty. The terrier mix glances at it, then looks away.

**Narrator:** "Every crime has a motive. Theirs was an empty bowl."

**On-screen text:** THE EMPTY BOWL

**Audio:** Heavy rain, distant thunder, one low piano note.

---

## Shot 2 — 00:12–00:24 — "The Suspects"

**Visual:** The terrier lies by the front door, chin on paws. The grey tabby sits on
the windowsill, tail flicking. Both stare at nothing.

**Narrator:** "Two suspects. No priors. No toys."

**On-screen text:** THE SUSPECTS

**Audio:** Rain continues, soft vinyl crackle, muted trumpet.

---

## Shot 3 — 00:24–00:36 — "First Offense"

**Visual:** The dog drags a single sock across the hallway floor. The cat bats a pencil
off a side table and watches it fall.

**Narrator:** "It began small. A sock. A pencil. Nobody got hurt."

**Audio:** Tense pizzicato strings under the rain.

---

## Shot 4 — 00:36–00:48 — "The Spree"

**Visual:** Unrolled toilet paper trails down the hallway. A cushion lies on the living
room floor; the cat stretches beside it, innocent. The dog trots past carrying a slipper.

**Narrator:** "Then it escalated. Paper. Cushions. A slipper that never came home."

**On-screen text:** GOOD PETS. BAD OPTIONS.

**Audio:** Strings build, rain heavier.

---

## Shot 5 — 00:48–01:00 — "The Pattern"

**Visual:** Close on the dog's eyes, then the cat's. Cut back to the empty bowl —
still empty.

**Narrator:** "Detectives look for patterns. The pattern was boredom."

**Audio:** Music drops to near silence. A single dripping faucet.

---

## Shot 6 — 01:00–01:12 — "The Delivery"

**Visual:** A cardboard box sits on the wet doorstep. A knock at the door. The dog's
ears lift. The cat appears at the edge of frame.

**Narrator:** "And then — a break in the case."

**Audio:** Door knock, rain, hopeful acoustic guitar enters.

---

## Shot 7 — 01:12–01:22 — "The Evidence"

**Visual:** The open box on the living room rug. Inside: a sturdy stick-shaped chew toy
(Durable Training Bite Stick Dog Toy) and a small rolling ball toy (PurrPlay
Self-Rolling Cat Toy Ball). The dog noses the chew toy; the cat paws at the ball toy.

**Narrator:** "The motive was never malice. It was an empty afternoon."

**On-screen text:** GOOD PETS. BAD OPTIONS.

**Audio:** Warm strings, rain fading.

---

## Shot 8 — 01:22–01:30 — "Case Closed"

**Visual:** The dog chews contentedly on the Durable Training Bite Stick Dog Toy. The cat
curls around the PurrPlay Self-Rolling Cat Toy Ball, eyes half closed. Sunlight breaks
through the window.

**Narrator:** "They were never bad. They were just bored. Who's A Good Boy."

**On-screen text:** WHO'S A GOOD BOY — whosagoodboypets.myshopify.com

**Audio:** Resolved chord, birdsong, rain gone.

---
